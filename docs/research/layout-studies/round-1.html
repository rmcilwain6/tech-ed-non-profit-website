Base layout · three directions

Layout studies

Three directions for the site's base layout, built with real content from the spec. Each one is a working mock-up: click through the navigation, switch themes, try phone width and tab through it with the keyboard.

The colours and fonts are stand-ins, chosen so the three are easy to tell apart. The brand work replaces them. Judge the structure: how pages are put together, how you find your way around, and how each would feel to a teacher or a student.

What all three already do
  • Navigation goes to separate pages and wraps onto a second line on phones. No hidden hamburger menu, so it works without JavaScript.
  • Skip link (press Tab inside the frame), visible focus, and a theme toggle that cycles System, Light and Dark.
  • [TK: …] placeholders wherever the spec has no confirmed copy, and labelled image slots at their final aspect ratio.
  • Motion uses transform and opacity only, and switches off with reduced motion.
  • Everything here is drawn by a script for the prototype. On the real site, the drawings (like the Transect profile) are generated at build time as static SVG.
