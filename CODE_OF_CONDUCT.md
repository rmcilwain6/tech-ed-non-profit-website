# Code of conduct

This project is run by a BC nonprofit that works with educators, students and communities. We expect the same care in this repository that we would bring to a classroom.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers, including people who are new to code, to GitHub, or to the topic.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind, and accept it the same way.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information, and never share anything about students.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the maintainers privately. Until the organization email exists, use GitHub's private reporting or contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or restrict participation, in response to behaviour that breaks this code.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
